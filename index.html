About DSAI 2027

The 5th International Conference on Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI2027) will be held on 10–12 November 2027. The conference brings together researchers, academics, industry professionals, and practitioners to advance data science and artificial intelligence through research, knowledge exchange, and international collaboration. With a focus on Responsible AI, DSAI2027 encourages advances that combine technical excellence with safety, fairness, transparency, and accountability.

Building on the conference’s commitment to supporting growth in developing countries, DSAI2027 provides a platform for strengthening research capacity, sharing expertise, and developing solutions that respond to local and global challenges. It connects established and emerging research communities, encouraging partnerships that support the inclusive and sustainable adoption of data science and AI.

The conference welcomes contributions across the foundations, methods, and applications of data science and artificial intelligence. DSAI2027 places particular emphasis on responsible development and deployment, including AI safety, risk assessment, robustness, human oversight, and the evaluation of potential societal impacts. These areas encourage discussion of how AI systems can remain reliable and beneficial throughout their lifecycle.

Authors are cordially invited to submit original research and share innovative methods, practical applications, and insights into the opportunities and challenges of data science and AI. Through presentations and academic discussions, participants will exchange ideas, establish collaborations, and explore future research directions.

We look forward to welcoming you to DSAI2027 as we work together to advance responsible, safe, and socially beneficial artificial intelligence.

Conference Topics

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  1. Machine Learning and Adaptive Intelligence

    • Deep and representation learning
    • Reinforcement and evolutionary learning
    • Intelligent agents and multi-agent systems
    • Hybrid and neuro-symbolic AI
    • Optimization for AI systems

  2. Data Science and Intelligent Analytics

    • Data mining and knowledge discovery
    • Predictive and prescriptive analytics
    • Graph, network, and pattern analytics
    • Decision support and AI-driven analytics
    • Data engineering for AI applications

  3. Trustworthy and Human-Centered AI

    • Explainable, fair, and transparent AI
    • Ethical AI and governance
    • Privacy-preserving and secure AI
    • Human-AI interaction and collaboration
    • Responsible AI deployment

  4. Language, Vision and Multimodal Intelligence

    • Natural language processing and large language models
    • Information retrieval and text analytics
    • Computer vision and image understanding
    • Multimodal learning and perception
    • Affective computing and sentiment analysis

  5. Applied and Domain-Specific AI

    • AI for education and healthcare
    • AI for smart cities and transportation
    • AI for business, industry, and government
    • AI for sustainability and environmental systems

  6. Knowledge, Reasoning and Semantic Systems

    • Knowledge representation and ontologies
    • Semantic technologies and linked data
    • Probabilistic and logical reasoning
    • Planning, scheduling, and constraint solving
    • Explainable and argumentation-based reasoning

  7. Responsible AI and AI Safety

    • Responsible AI design and lifecycle management
    • AI safety evaluation, benchmarking, and red teaming
    • AI risk assessment and societal impact assessment
    • Robustness, reliability, and adversarial resilience
    • AI alignment, controllability, and human oversight
    • Safety of generative AI, foundation models, and autonomous agents
    • Formal verification and safety assurance for AI systems
    • Monitoring, incident response, and mitigation of AI harms
